# Chat interjections: residue when they answer a request, normal in dialogue and mail.
# Reported only at the start of a block, a table cell, a line or a sentence (after . ! ? or :), so "faster, that is
# for sure!" and "a great question to ask your vendor" are not. Not reported for --doc-type script,
# essay or mail, or inside double quotation marks ("..." or “...”); single quotation marks are not
# treated as dialogue, because ' and ’ are also apostrophes.
