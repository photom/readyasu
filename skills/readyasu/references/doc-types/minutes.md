# Minutes (minutes)

See also: domains/business.md
Lint rules exempted: bold_label_list, excess_list, fragment_run

## 1. Normal forms (do not change)

- Labels and values (`**Date**: May 9`, `Attendees: ...`), short fragments ("Decided: ship on Friday").
- Headings for agenda items, decisions and action items. Do not change these headings.
- Owner names next to action items.

## 2. Still fix

- Who said or decided what when the minutes are ambiguous: ask, do not guess.
- Decisions recorded as discussion, or discussion recorded as decisions: keep the original status and ask.
- Stock phrases ("A productive discussion was had").

## 3. Length and omissions

- Keep every decision, action item, owner and due date.

## 4. Examples (synthetic)

| Original | Handling |
|---|---|
| - **Action**: Kim to update the runbook by 5/12 | Keep |
| The team had a robust discussion around pricing. | "The team discussed pricing." |
