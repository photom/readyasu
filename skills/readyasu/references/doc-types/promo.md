# Feature articles and marketing copy (promo)

See also: domains/business.md
Lint rules exempted: emoji, exclamation, fragment_run, self_answered_question

## 1. Normal forms (do not change)

- Energy, taglines, short punchy runs, calls to action, exclamation marks, emoji the brand uses.
- "The result?" and similar hooks.
- Bold heading lines.
- Lively verbs that are the brand's voice; metaphor_verb findings are still reviewed, and you keep the energy while saying what the product does when the text says it.

## 2. Still fix

- Claims without support ("the fastest", "guaranteed results"): do not change the body; ask.
- Stock AI vocabulary that sounds generated ("elevate your experience", "unlock your potential") when a concrete benefit is in the text.
- Stacked superlatives ("truly revolutionary, game-changing innovation"): keep them in the body and ask what supports the claims.

## 3. Length and omissions

- Keep every feature, price and condition (including fine print).

## 4. Examples (synthetic)

| Original | Handling |
|---|---|
| Faster. Lighter. Yours. | Keep |
| Unlock seamless productivity with our cutting-edge AI. | Keep the sentence and ask what supports "cutting-edge" and "seamless"; rewrite only with what the text says ("Let our cutting-edge AI draft your replies for seamless productivity" if drafting replies is the feature) |
