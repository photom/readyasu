# Proposal (proposal)

See also: domains/business.md
Lint rules exempted: none

## 1. Normal forms (do not change)

- Problem, options, recommendation, cost and the decision requested.
- Option tables and pros/cons lists.
- Hedges about estimates ("roughly", "we estimate").

## 2. Still fix

- Inflated benefits ("transformative", "game-changing") when the proposal gives the numbers.
- Recommendations written as facts: keep "we recommend" as a recommendation.
- Vague asks: keep the decision requested and its deadline in the same sentence.

## 3. Length and omissions

- Keep every option, including rejected ones, and every cost figure.

## 4. Examples (synthetic)

| Original | Handling |
|---|---|
| This bold initiative will revolutionize our onboarding. | "This initiative will change our onboarding by ..." from the proposal's own content (keep "will" and "our"); ask about the claim if nothing supports it |
