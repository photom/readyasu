# Research paper and report (paper)

See also: domains/tech.md
Lint rules exempted: none

## 1. Normal forms (do not change)

- Hedges ("suggests", "may indicate", "is consistent with"); never remove them.
- "Significant" in the statistical sense, "robust" in the statistical or engineering sense, "state of the art" when compared with cited work.
- Passive voice in methods ("Samples were collected"), "we" for the authors.
- Citations in any style; keep their form and position.

## 2. Still fix

- Stock vocabulary in the introduction and conclusion ("delve", "pivotal", "a testament to").
- Causal claims added to correlations: keep "associated with", do not write "caused".
- Inflated novelty ("groundbreaking") without support: ask.

## 3. Length and omissions

- Keep every number, unit, sample size, p-value and citation.

## 4. Examples (synthetic)

| Original | Handling |
|---|---|
| These results suggest a possible link between X and Y [3]. | Keep |
| This groundbreaking study delves into the pivotal role of sleep. | "This groundbreaking study examines the pivotal role of sleep in ..." from the paper's own question (only "delves into" changes); ask what supports "groundbreaking" and "pivotal" |
