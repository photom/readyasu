# Formal documents (formal)

See also: domains/business.md
Lint rules exempted: none

## 1. Normal forms (do not change)

- "Shall", "must", "may" in their defined senses; defined terms with capitals.
- Numbered clauses, cross-references, "in the event that", "prior to" when used consistently.
- Passive voice for obligations whose actor is defined elsewhere.

## 2. Still fix

- Stock AI vocabulary in non-legal parts (introductions, summaries).
- Inconsistent terms that the document does not define as the same: ask.
- Never change "shall" to "will", "must" to "should", or "may" to "can".

## 3. Length and omissions

- Keep every clause, number and cross-reference. Keep the number, order and numbering of clauses; do not split or join clauses.

## 4. Examples (synthetic)

| Original | Handling |
|---|---|
| The Licensee shall notify the Licensor within 30 days. | Keep |
| This policy seamlessly fosters a culture of security. | Keep the sentence and ask what the policy does to foster it ("seamlessly" included) unless the same or next sentence says so; do not replace the claim with a rule from elsewhere in the document |
