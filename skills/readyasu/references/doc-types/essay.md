# Essay and fiction (essay)

See also: domains/essay.md
Lint rules exempted: fragment_run, exclamation, chat_residue (interjections only)

## 1. Normal forms (do not change)

- First person, the writer's voice, rhythm, deliberate fragments and repetition.
- Metaphors the reader can follow (slop-catalog.md section 8).
- Ellipses and commas used as the writer's style. Dashes stay only in fiction narration and dialogue; in essays, rewrite clause-splicing dashes (SKILL.md "Sentence length and punctuation").
- Dialogue in fiction, including "Of course." as an answer.

## 2. Still fix

- Metaphors that do not hold ("the afternoon hugged its knees"), and metaphorical verbs on ordinary or technical events (lint reports them as metaphor_verb).
- Inflated words for small experiences (slop-catalog.md section 4).
- Two-sentence negative contrast in essay prose (not in fiction narration or dialogue).
- Morals and lessons at the end: keep the content and tone down only the inflated words (SKILL.md "Top rule"). Delete a stock moral only in a draft you wrote yourself (fact-recovery.md section 2, step 1) when nothing earlier supports it.
- Do not add feelings the writer did not state.
- Do not fill facts from the conversation or files, and do not ask fact questions (fact-recovery.md section 6).

## 3. Length and omissions

- Keep the paragraphing and the ending (except a stock moral deleted under section 2).

## 4. Examples (synthetic)

| Original | Handling |
|---|---|
| The coffee was a symphony of quiet hope. | Keep the sentence and ask what the writer meant, unless the essay itself says what the hope was about; then rewrite with those words, keeping the subject (do not add "I") |
| I closed the laptop. Done. | Keep |
