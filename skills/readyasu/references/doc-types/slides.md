# Slides (slides)

See also: domains/business.md
Lint rules exempted: bold_label_list, excess_list, fragment_run, self_answered_question, count_announcement, closing_summary
Also not reported in slides: "key takeaways" (a summary slide) and "thank you for your attention" (the closing slide).

## 1. Normal forms (do not change)

- Short fragments, one idea per line, headings that state the point.
- Labels and values, "Three reasons" with three bullets, "Why now?" headings, a summary slide.
- Slide separators (`---`) and speaker notes.

## 2. Still fix

- Full paragraphs on a slide when the request asks for slides.
- Emoji used as bullets, stock vocabulary, inflated claims.
- Counts that do not match the bullets: ask.

## 3. Length and omissions

- Keep the number and order of slides.

## 4. Examples (synthetic)

| Original | Handling |
|---|---|
| ## Why now? | Keep |
| - 🚀 Supercharge velocity | "- Ship faster" only if the slide's notes say what changes; otherwise remove only the emoji and ask |
