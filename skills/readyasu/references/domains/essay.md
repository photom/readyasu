# Essays and fiction

## 1. Voice
- The writer's voice comes first. Keep rhythm, sentence fragments, repetition and punctuation used as style (not clause-splicing dashes; SKILL.md "Sentence length and punctuation").
- Rewrite stock AI vocabulary and inflated words for small experiences (slop-catalog.md sections 3 and 4).

## 2. Metaphors
- Keep metaphors the reader can follow; rewrite only those that do not hold, and metaphorical verbs on ordinary or technical events (slop-catalog.md section 8).
- Do not add metaphors.

## 3. Feelings and lessons
- Do not add feelings or lessons the writer did not state.
- A closing that turns the story into a stock moral: keep the content and tone down only the inflated words; delete it only in a draft you wrote yourself when nothing in the essay leads to it.

## 4. Dialogue
- Keep dialogue as written, including stock phrases a character would say.
