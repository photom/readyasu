# Essays and fiction

## 1. Voice
- The writer's voice comes first. Keep rhythm, sentence fragments, repetition and punctuation used as style.
- Rewrite stock AI vocabulary and inflated words for small experiences (slop-catalog.md sections 3 and 4).

## 2. Metaphors
- Keep metaphors the reader can follow; rewrite only those that do not hold (slop-catalog.md section 8).
- Do not add metaphors.

## 3. Feelings and lessons
- Do not add feelings or lessons the writer did not state.
- A closing that turns the story into a stock moral ("In the end, it taught me that every journey matters") goes only when nothing in the essay leads to it; otherwise keep it and tone down the inflated words.

## 4. Dialogue
- Keep dialogue as written, including stock phrases a character would say.
