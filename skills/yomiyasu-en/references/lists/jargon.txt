# Corporate jargon. Say the plain action when the original says what it is.
circle back	"follow up", with when and about what
touch base	"talk", "check in"
move the needle	say what changes
low-hanging fruit	"easy fixes", name them if given
bandwidth	"time", "capacity" (keep network bandwidth)
drive alignment	name who agrees on what
north star	name the goal
double-click on	"look at in detail"
take this offline	"discuss separately"
boil the ocean	"take on too much"
best-in-class	state the comparison
value-add	say what value
paradigm shift	state the change
