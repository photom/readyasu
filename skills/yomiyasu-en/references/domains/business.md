# Business writing

## 1. Requests and decisions
- Put the request, the owner and the deadline in the same sentence.
- Keep decisions and recommendations distinct; do not turn "we recommend" into "we will".

## 2. Numbers
- Keep amounts, currencies, dates and percentages exactly. Do not round.
- Keep the hedges on estimates ("about", "up to").

## 3. Corporate jargon
- "circle back", "move the needle", "low-hanging fruit", "bandwidth" (for capacity), "synergy", "drive alignment", "deep dive" (outside meeting names): rewrite to the plain action when the original says what it is; otherwise keep and ask.
- Keep terms the organization defines (OKRs, a project code name).

## 4. Politeness
- See register.md. Do not add apologies, thanks or flattery.
