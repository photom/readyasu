# Manual (manual)

See also: domains/tech.md
Lint rules exempted: excess_list

## 1. Normal forms (do not change)

- Numbered steps, one action per step, imperative mood ("Click Save").
- Notes, warnings and cautions in the document's existing format.
- Prerequisites before the steps. UI labels exactly as they appear.

## 2. Still fix

- Steps hidden in parentheses: list them in the order they are done.
- Personified software ("the app will gladly remember your settings").
- Signposting ("Now let's move on to the exciting part").

## 3. Length and omissions

- Keep every step, value and warning. Do not merge steps.
