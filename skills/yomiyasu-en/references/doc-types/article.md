# Technical article (article)

See also: domains/tech.md
Lint rules exempted: none

## 1. Normal forms (do not change)

- Code blocks, inline code, command output and their captions.
- A short introduction that states what the article covers.
- Second person ("you") when the article guides the reader.

## 2. Still fix

- Openers such as "In today's fast-paced world" and "Let's dive in".
- Closing sections that only repeat the body ("Conclusion", "Final thoughts") unless they add a next step or a limitation.
- Bold on random phrases, bold labels with colons, emoji in headings.
- Personified code ("the function knows", "the compiler is unhappy") unless it is a common phrase that cannot be misread ("the compiler complains" is fine).

## 3. Length and omissions

- Keep every step and every caveat.
- Keep headings in the same order.

## 4. Examples (synthetic)

| Original | Handling |
|---|---|
| In this article, we'll dive deep into Rust's borrow checker. | "This article explains how Rust's borrow checker works." |
| The borrow checker is your friend, not your enemy. | Keep if the article argues against the common frustration; otherwise "The borrow checker catches ..." from the article's own content |
